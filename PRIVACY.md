# Privacy Policy for Kairo

**Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

Kairo ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices regarding the **Kairo** desktop application.

---

## 1. Local-First Data Storage
Kairo is built with a **local-first architecture**:
- All notes, folders, tags, attachments, images, and user preferences are stored **locally on your device** in a local SQLite database and application data directory.
- We do **not** upload, store, process, or sync your notes to any remote servers controlled by us.

---

## 2. No Personal Data Collection & No Telemetry
- **Zero Data Collection:** Kairo does **not** collect, harvest, or track any personal identification information (such as your name, email address, IP address, or location).
- **No Analytics / Telemetry:** Kairo does **not** include third-party analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or usage telemetry software.

---

## 3. Application Updates
- Kairo uses standard public GitHub Release API endpoints (`https://api.github.com/repos/Kairo-md/kairo-releases`) solely to verify if a newer application version is available.
- No personal user content or data is transmitted during update checks.

---

## 4. Third-Party Links & Content
If you insert external hyperlinks or media embeds into your notes, clicking or interacting with those external links will be handled by your web browser subject to their respective privacy policies.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect application improvements or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted directly in this file within the application repository.
